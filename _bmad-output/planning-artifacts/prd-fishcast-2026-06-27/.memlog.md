- (decision) Core idea: probabilistic forecasts of salmon harvest for WA sport fisheries (freshwater + Puget Sound marine), serving both WDFW managers and the angling public
- (decision) Data sources: WA data.wa.gov open data portal (creel data, token/throttling needed); existing WDFW prior art at github.com/wdfw-fp/CreelEstimates (freshwater point-estimate + Bayesian state-space methods) and github.com/wdfw-fp/creelutils (R utility/ETL package)
- (decision) Environmental predictors in scope: NOAA 7-day weather forecast, USGS/NOAA river flow, NOAA tide predictions (marine)
- (assumption) Open question: do existing WDFW creel repos (freshwater-only) cover marine/Puget Sound creel data, or is that a separate data source/process?
- (decision) Stakes calibration: hobby/personal project. Working mode: Coaching path, Journey-led entry point
- (decision) UJ-1 (Mark Smith, Statewide Salmon & Steelhead Manager): monitors TAC/harvest control rule status (esp. ESA-listed Puget Sound Chinook, steelhead), catch trend vs angler success/satisfaction, and other-species (Chinook/Coho/Sockeye) tracking vs FMP guidelines. Needs total estimated harvest, released fish, total encounters, CPUE, total angler effort. Outcomes: inform status, decide keep-open/close/extend a fishery, coordinate/share with tribal co-managers
- (decision) UJ-2 (TrollRay, weekend salmon angler): for a given fishery (e.g. Skagit sockeye 2026), wants reported catch-to-date (raw), estimated harvest-to-date (statistical expansion), and forward harvest projection (days/week ahead) in one view, to decide whether to fish today/this weekend/within the week. 'Odds of success' = overall harvest-level visibility for MVP (angler infers own odds); personal catch-probability (CPUE-derived) is a future goal, not MVP
- (decision) Form factor: web app (browser dashboard) for MVP. Concerns confirmed: regulatory/compliance sensitivity (ESA-listed stock thresholds), third-party data dependency (data.wa.gov rate limits, NOAA/USGS APIs), multi-stakeholder coordination (tribal co-managers need shareable output)
- (decision) Features confirmed as separate views: Data Ingestion & Harmonization, Harvest Estimation Model, Manager View, Angler View (not a combined dashboard)
- (decision) Added feature: Forecast Accuracy Tracking, user-facing (predicted vs actual harvest, tracked over time, visible to managers/anglers). Drives primary Success Metric on forecast accuracy
